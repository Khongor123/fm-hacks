# F&MHacks 2026 judges’ guide

**For the six appointed judges only.** This guide covers account setup and project review, not general event operations. The website currently grants judges the technical **organizer** role so they can open submissions. Judges should use `/admin/submissions` for review and leave invitations, the registration roster, and participant check-in to the organizing team.

## 1. Get your invitation

An event organizer will verify your identity and share a private invitation link directly with you. The link is tied to **one F&M email address**, works **once**, and expires **seven days** after it is created. The website does not email invitations automatically. Do not forward your link to another judge or anyone else.

Use the exact F&M address the organizer invited. If the link has expired, was revoked, or has already been used, ask the organizer for a new one. Do not create an account through the public `/register` page for judge access; that page creates a participant account.

## 2. Activate your account

### If you do not have an F&MHacks account

1. Open the invitation link the organizer sent you.
2. Under **New to F&MHacks?**, enter your full name, the invited F&M email address, and a new password of at least eight characters.
3. Select **Create organizer account**. The invitation is checked when your account is created. The site may land on `/admin` because it uses the organizer role for judges.
4. Select **Review submissions** there, or go directly to `/admin/submissions`. The other `/admin` controls are for the organizing team.

You do not need to enter a class year, T-shirt size, or attendance commitment. An invited judge account can be created even after public participant registration closes, and it does not take one of the 50 participant places.

### If you already have an F&MHacks account

1. Open the invitation link and use **Have an account?** to sign in with the invited F&M email and your existing password.
2. The site checks the invitation and changes your account to an organizer account. If you are already signed in, check the email shown on the invitation page and select **Accept invitation**. If it shows a different account, log out and return to the link with the invited account.
3. Once accepted, select **Review submissions** on `/admin`.

### Sign in again later

Go to `/login` and use the same F&M email and password. Then open `/admin/submissions` directly. If you forget your password, use **Forgot password** on the login page. Contact an event organizer if the reset email does not arrive.

## 3. Find the projects

The **Project submissions** page at `/admin/submissions` groups entries under **AI**, **Social good**, and **Creative build**. Each section shows a count and its project cards. The page includes both team projects and solo projects. A team card shows its team name; a solo card is labeled **Solo project**.

The page shows **submitted projects only**. Participant drafts are private and do not appear in the track counts or project cards. A submitted project can still be revised and resubmitted before the deadline, so refresh before final judging.

Each card shows:

- The project title and description.
- Team members or the solo participant, with each person's arrival status.
- A GitHub repository link, when provided, and whether the participant affirmed it is public.
- The declared AI-use level and disclosure.
- **Download slides** when a slide file has been uploaded; otherwise **None uploaded**.
- The last update time, displayed in Eastern Time.

## 4. Review a submitted project

1. Start with a card marked **submitted** in the assigned track. Read its title, description, and member list.
2. Open the **GitHub** link. Confirm that it opens and that the repository is publicly accessible. The **Public link affirmed: yes** label records the participant's statement; it does not verify the link. If necessary, check it in a signed-out or private browser window.
3. Select **Download slides ↗** to view the presentation. Slide files are kept in private storage and download to your device; they are not public links. Accepted uploads are `.pptx`, `.key`, or `.pdf` files of up to 10 MiB. Keep downloaded files within the judging group.
4. Read the **AI use** level and disclosure alongside the project. Use the judging rubric supplied separately to the six judges; the website does not provide a scoring or feedback form.
5. Refresh the page before making final decisions, especially near the deadline, to see the latest project details.

Submissions open **November 7, 2026 at 10:00 a.m. Eastern** and close **November 8 at 10:00 a.m. Eastern**. Participants can revise and resubmit during that window. After it closes, they can correct the GitHub URL on an existing entry, so recheck a link if an organizer tells you it was updated.

## If something is missing or fails

- **Invitation rejected:** Confirm that you used the invited F&M email. Ask the organizer for a fresh link if the original expired, was revoked, or was already used.
- **No access to submissions:** Sign in with the account that accepted the invitation. If `/admin/submissions` sends you to `/account`, ask an organizer to confirm that your account is marked as an organizer.
- **No project in a track:** The page may say **No submissions in this track**. Check the other sections and confirm your assigned track with the judging organizers.
- **GitHub link absent or inaccessible:** Note the issue for the organizers. Do not assume the public-link affirmation means the repository works.
- **Slides unavailable:** Refresh and try **Download slides** again. If it still fails, tell an organizer which project is affected.
- **Submissions could not be loaded:** Refresh once, then ask an organizer for help if the message remains.

Your account can technically open other private organizer pages, but those are outside the judges' role. Do not use the roster, invitation controls, or check-in controls. Keep your password and downloaded slides private, and share project materials only through the agreed judging process.
